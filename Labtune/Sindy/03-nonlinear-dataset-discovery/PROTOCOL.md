## Required distinctions and future-test gates

Positive controls must not be mislabeled as actual anomaly datasets. A physics-chosen cubic mechanism can motivate Silverbox modeling, but stronger nonlinear prediction alone does not establish fault detection. Ordinary excitation/amplitude/operating changes are not automatically anomalies.

All future comparators must receive identical causal observables, forcing inputs, regimes and split information. Include strong linear ARX/VAR, persistence/static controls where applicable, a dense nonlinear predictor and a sparse correction with a genuine null option. Preserve zero-term, constant-predictor and no-advantage outcomes.

Use group/time/cycle-safe splits. Model and threshold selection belong to development; untouched confirmation cannot become another search panel. Sampling, observed inputs/states, excitation, labels and license gates must close before freezing a numerical protocol. These outputs are protocol outlines, not ready-to-run frozen scientific experiments.
